## Up to 100 units per year

The same individual or legal entity may sell **up to and including 100
covered units per calendar year**, worldwide, without a license fee or an
individual authorization request.

A calendar year runs from January 1 through December 31. Count together all
sales by that individual or entity across its brands, stores, sales channels
and product variants. Count each unit in the year its sale is completed.

A covered unit is one complete Mickey Hub device, one device substantially
derived from Mickey Hub, or one kit for assembling such a device. A bundle
containing several devices or kits counts each separately.

Within this allowance, you may use, reproduce, modify and distribute the
covered work for commercial activities associated with those units, including
designing, manufacturing, commissioning manufacture, marketing and selling
them. This includes the associated first-party firmware and software tools.

**Obtain a separate written commercial license before the sale that would
